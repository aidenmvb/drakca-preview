Financing

A way to pay for the work.

For an improvement or a repair, you can check loan offers through Acorn Finance before or after the office writes the estimate. The application opens on Acorn’s site and is tied to this office.

Financing applies to the improvement or repair. Rent stays on the lease. The loan comes from a lending partner. Drakca writes the estimate. The lender sets the rate and the approval.
