Financing

Financing for the work.

Some improvement and repair projects can be financed through Acorn Finance. You can review options before or after we prepare an estimate. The application opens on Acorn’s site. Approval is not guaranteed, and financing is not available for every project or borrower.

Financing applies to the improvement or repair, not to rent. The loan comes from a lending partner. DRAKA writes the estimate and does not lend. The lender sets the rate and the approval.

Check options with Acorn Finance

  • Personal loans from $1,000 to $100,000 for qualified borrowers
  • For home improvement expenses
  • Acorn states the first check is a soft credit inquiry

Check financing options

A lender may make a hard credit inquiry if you choose an offer. Terms, fees, and approval come from that lender. Acorn states the offer is available in the United States except Massachusetts. Questions about an application go to Acorn Finance at 916-404-1982.
