Which buildings do you take?

Apartment houses, small mixed-use buildings, retail rows, modest offices, and the occasional house. The territory is the District of Columbia, suburban Maryland, and Northern Virginia. We do not take scattered condominiums across the country.

What does management cost?

It depends on the building: size, the age of the systems, and whether it is occupied. We quote after the walk-through, for that property.

Can you also sell it?

Yes. Brokerage sits with management. A sale is written into the scope when you want one. We represent buildings we know, including ones we already manage.
