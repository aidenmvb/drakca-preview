Based in Bethesda, DRAKA works with buyers, sellers, and property owners across Maryland, Virginia, and Washington, D.C. The work brings together brokerage, property management, and improvements and repairs.

DRAKA is the name of DL Management, a brokerage in Bethesda, Maryland.

Buying and selling

For a purchase, DRAKA handles search, valuation, and negotiation for residential and investment property in Maryland, Virginia, and the District. For a sale, the work is pricing, preparation, and marketing. An open listing shows the rent or sale price when one is posted.

Brokerage

Property management

Management covers houses, apartments, and mixed-use property. Services and fees are tailored after a consultation. A scope can include leasing and renewals, maintenance and capital planning, rent, payables, and monthly statements, resident communication, and sales representation when an owner is ready to sell.

For owners

Improvements and repairs

An estimate covers a home, a business, or a rental property. The work is quoted and billed separately from rent. The property is assessed before the estimate, and you see the written scope before work starts. Financing for that work may be available through Acorn Finance.

How we work

Work starts with a conversation. An owner shares the property’s location, occupancy, and what is needed. That first conversation does not require a retainer. A buyer, a seller, or someone planning an improvement describes the property and the requirements.

DRAKA then assesses the property or those requirements. Management uses a walk-through to review condition, rental information, and the local requirements that apply. A rent opinion follows the inspection. An improvement or repair is assessed before an estimate is prepared.

The scope is agreed from that assessment. Management can cover leasing, maintenance, accounting, and reporting, with services and fees tailored after the consultation. Improvements and repairs are a written estimate you see before any work starts. No occupancy is guaranteed in advance.
