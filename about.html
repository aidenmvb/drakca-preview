About

Local perspective. A broader view of real estate.

Based in Bethesda, Drakca works with buyers, sellers, and property owners across Maryland, Virginia, and Washington, D.C. Our work brings together brokerage, property management, and improvements and repairs.

DRAKA is the name of DL Management, a brokerage in Bethesda, Maryland.

What we do

Buying and selling

For a purchase, DRAKA handles search, valuation, and negotiation for residential and investment property in Maryland, Virginia, and the District. For a sale, the work is pricing, preparation, and marketing. Guidance on buying and selling investment property is part of this practice. An open listing shows the rent or sale price when one is posted.

Brokerage

Property management

Management covers houses, apartments, and mixed-use property. Services and fees are tailored to the property after a consultation. A scope can include leasing and renewals, maintenance and capital planning, rent, payables, and monthly statements, resident communication, and sales representation when an owner is ready to sell.

For owners

Improvements and repairs

An estimate is available for improvements and repairs at a home, a business, or a rental property. The work is quoted and billed separately from rent. DRAKA assesses the property before preparing the estimate, and you see the written scope before work starts. Financing for that work may be available through Acorn Finance.

How we work

An initial conversation

Share the property’s location, occupancy, and what you need. The first conversation does not require a retainer. A buyer, a seller, or someone planning an improvement uses the same step to describe the property and the requirements.

An assessment

DRAKA then assesses the property or those requirements. For management, a walk-through reviews condition, rental information, and the local requirements that apply, and a rent opinion follows the inspection. An improvement or repair is assessed before an estimate is prepared. Brokerage reviews the search, the pricing, or the property an owner is bringing to market.

An agreed scope

The scope is agreed from that assessment. Management can cover leasing, maintenance, accounting, and reporting, with services and fees tailored after the consultation. Brokerage follows the search or the sale described in the conversation. Improvements and repairs are a written estimate you see before any work starts. No occupancy is guaranteed in advance.
