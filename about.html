Buying and selling

For a purchase, the work is search, valuation, and negotiation for residential and investment property in Maryland, Virginia, and the District.

For a sale, the work is pricing, preparation, and marketing. An open listing shows the rent or sale price when one is posted.

Explore brokerage services

Property management

Management covers houses, apartments, and mixed-use property. Services and fees are tailored after a consultation.

A scope can include leasing and renewals, maintenance and capital planning, rent, payables, monthly statements, resident communication, and sales representation when an owner is ready to sell. A walk-through reviews condition, rental information, and the local requirements that apply. A rent opinion follows the inspection. No occupancy is guaranteed in advance.

Discuss property management

Improvements and repairs

An estimate covers a home, a business, or a rental property. The work is quoted and billed separately from rent.

The property is assessed before the estimate, and you see the written scope before work starts. Financing for that work may be available through Acorn Finance.

How we work

A conversation

Share the property’s location, occupancy, and what is needed. A buyer, a seller, or someone planning an improvement describes the property and the requirements. The first conversation does not require a retainer.

An assessment

The property or those requirements are assessed before a scope is set.

An agreed scope

The scope is agreed from that assessment.
